# Stage 2 Reflection

During this stage, I developed the requirements for the SmartCare system
before using AI to review my work. The AI helped me notice that some of my
requirements were too broad or ambiguous. For example, FR-04 stated that
the system should prevent duplicate appointment bookings, but I had not
clearly defined what should count as a duplicate. The client brief confirms
that duplicate bookings are a problem, but it does not provide the exact
rules for identifying them.

The AI also identified that terms such as "reliably" and "simple and
understandable" in my non-functional requirements would be difficult to
test. However, I could not simply add measurable targets because these had
not been provided by the client.

One AI suggestion that needed to be modified concerned cancelled
appointments. The AI raised the possibility of keeping cancelled
appointments in the appointment history, but this behaviour was not
confirmed in the client brief. I therefore kept this as an open question
rather than turning it into a requirement.

This activity showed me why requirements need evidence. AI can help identify
missing information and ambiguity, but its suggestions should not
automatically become requirements. Requirements should be supported by the
client brief or confirmed with the client before they are included.
